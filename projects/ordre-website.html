The atelier

Fewer garments.
Made properly.

ORDRE makes a small number of cycling garments and makes them well. Every piece is patterned for the riding position, sewn in limited runs and repaired for free for as long as you own it.

We do not discount, and we do not chase seasons. A Monocoque jersey from Collection 01 should still be the one you reach for in five years.
